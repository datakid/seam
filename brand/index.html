Seam

Repairs damaged Word, Excel and PowerPoint files in the browser. The name comes from the line where two torn edges are joined. The icon shows exactly that: one sheet, torn across, held together by a single chromatic line.

Seam

The seam spectrum

Full-chroma colour appears in one place only: the seam line (header rule, progress, the icon). Everything else uses soft tints of it.
